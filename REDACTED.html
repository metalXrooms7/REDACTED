>>1.0  CASE SUMMERY

On 09 SEPTEMBER 1996, field operatives attached to Region 10 recovered irregular biological remains along the EAST TRAIL hiking grounds within GRAYS HARBOR COUNTY, WASHINGTON (44.7∘ N,123.9∘ W). Standard post-mortem decay processes appear to be COMPLETELY ABSENT in the recovered casualty. In place of expected putrefaction, the specimen exhibits total systemic desiccation consistent with no known terrestrial taphonomic mechanism. The recovered specimen is entirely dry; despite severe, massive trauma to the thoracic region, no blood volume remains on the clothing or the surrounding terrain. Investigation of the cranial cavity revealed the optic nerves and eyes are entirely absent, leaving only completely hollow, empty sockets. Physical composition of the epidermis has altered significantly, presenting not as decaying organic matter, but as a rigid, hollowed shell—resembling a physical abomination wearing tightly drawn skin rather than a natural corpse. The Office hereby designates the affected area as CONTAMINATION ZONE 0042-X pending full site quarantine and retrieval of associated personal electronic evidence.

It must be EXPLICITLY noted, for purposes of this report, that even in isolated liquid form obtained via high-velocity saline flush of the host's dry vasculature, and completely absent any remaining host tissue, electrical source, or ambient excitation the microscopic crystals suspended in the fluid continue to emit a high-intensity, self-sustaining purple/magenta luminescence.The origin of this glow remains undetermined, though its active radiation is suspected to correlate directly with the rapid cellular desiccation of the victim's skin. Personnel are advised to handle all liquid extraction samples with opaque containment vessels and to limit direct visual exposure to no more than 90 seconds per shift to avoid severe ocular strain.

>>3.0 HYPOTHESIS (PRELIMINARY)

The Office currently maintains the working position that the contamination represents the byproduct of an UNLICENSED CHEMICAL MANUFACTURING OPERATION likely a clandestine industrial facility dumping synthetic agents and unrefined industrial slurry into the deep woods bordering the East Trail. No alternative origin is considered. Inquiries regarding cosmic, extraterrestrial, or otherwise extra-jurisdictional sourcing are NOT ENTERTAINED at this time and shall be referred for administrative review. Of greater operational concern: the agent displays an AGGRESSIVE, PREDATORY REPLICATION CYCLE upon direct contact with organic tissue. Inoculation studies on porcine subjects (PIG-A through PIG-D) demonstrate complete desiccation and rigid epidermal hardening of soft tissue within 41 to 86 minutes post-exposure. The agent appears to actively seek vascular pathways, entirely evacuating all biological fluids while proliferating microscopic crystals along the inner walls of the bloodstream. This rapid process leaves the deceased subject as a completely dry, hollowed-out corpse with tightly drawn skin.

>> 4.0 SOCIAL VECTOR

Field surveillance reports indicate that a loose ONLINE ADOLESCENT AFFILIATE GROUP — observed posting on bulletin board systems and USENET groups including alt.glow.east, alt.binaries.contam, and rec.outdoor.washington  has begun treating the contamination site with apparent RELIGIOUS SIGNIFICANCE. The group is reported to be manufacturing and distributing HIGH-DENSITY OPTICAL MEDIA DISKS (Compact Disc Recordable, 650MB) containing manifesto text, low-resolution photographic plates of the affected trail, and short uncompressed audio recordings, by mail, in unmarked sleeves. Notably, field recovery units have reported individual disks left directly at discovery sites adjacent to recovered casualties, often marked with singular handwritten designations such as "SA."or “Sonic Adventure.”

Counter-distribution and seizure operations are pending interagency coordination. Schools and local public libraries within Grays Harbor County have been advised — without disclosure of cause — to flag any unsolicited optical media received by mail.

>> A. PRELIMINARY NOTE

The following transcript covers interview sessions conducted on the evening of recovery, in a mobile field unit parked at the Grays Harbor County ranger station. PARTY ONE (P1) is identified as a Senior Recovery Specialist. PARTY TWO (P2) is a county park ranger who was first to report the anomaly and locate the civilian remains on the East Trail. PARTY THREE (P3), introduced in Session 2, is the attending medical examiner reviewing the systemic desiccation, missing blood volume, and vacated cranial orbits of the casualty. All names have been redacted by request of the Office of General Counsel.

>> B. SESSION 1 – 21:14 PST

P1: Tape rolling. State your name and position for the record.

P2: Ranger, twelve years on East Trail. Name —--------.

P1: Walk me through what you found on the morning of the ninth.

P2: I came up the east fork around five-thirty. Spotted something unusual a good distance off the trail, out in a clearing. At first I thought it was just discarded gear, but as I got closer, the shape of it looked completely wrong. It was entirely still.

P1: Wrong. Explain.

P2: The positioning of the body. It was clear it hadn't dropped there naturally. It looked like it had been dragged a massive distance—easily a mile away from the tree line.

P1: (Consults case notes) A mile out from the tree line. That matches our timeline. The initial scene was much closer to the old mill.

P2: I approached. About ten feet out I saw the civilian. The level of mutilation was severe. The chest cavity was completely —----- —---, but the wound was entirely dry—no blood left on the clothes or the ground, and no smell. His eyes were entirely gone—just completely hollow, empty sockets.

P1: Completely —------- ---. That lines up exactly with the description from a missing person's emergency dispatch call we intercepted back on January 3rd. We finally found him.

P2: Did you? I didn't know how long he'd been out there.

P1: Did you make contact with the remains?

P2: No, sir. I did not.

P1: Good.

P2: His cell phone was dropped right next to him in the dirt. It looked like it had been sitting out there in the elements for months, screen completely shattered.

P1: We will be retrieving that. It likely contains the original recording.

P2: It's not on me. I didn't touch it.

P1: Stop the tape.

>> C. SESSION 2 – 22:48 PST

P1: Resuming. Doctor, please describe the condition of the remains as recovered.

P3: In thirty-one years of practice I have never seen tissue do this. The musculature is not necrotic—it is completely dehydrated. Drained. Every ounce of biological fluid has been entirely evacuated from the major vasculature, but there's no sign of natural decomposition. The skin has hardened into this rigid, stretched shell.

P1: What caused the illumination reported at the scene?

P3: The circulatory system. When we opened the vessels to inspect the deep pathology, the inner walls were completely coated in microscopic bits of —------. They are suspended inside the dry lining of the arteries. Under the lights, you can see it glowing. A high-intensity, self-sustaining purple or magenta luminescence.

P1: Doctor, please stick to clinical language.

P3: My apologies. The agent demonstrates a predatory replication cycle with an extreme affinity for hemoglobin-bearing pathways. It floods the bloodstream, aggressively forces out all standard moisture, and multiplies at a sub-micron scale. I have isolated a fluid suspension sample from the vascular flush. In a sealed jar. In the dark. It is glowing, agent. In the dark.

P1: Containment?

P3: Opaque containment vessels. Absolutely no light exposure. I would feel better if we limited direct visual exposure to no more than ninety seconds per shift. The ocular strain is severe.

P1: Have you observed any pattern in the internal contamination?

P3: Yes. The micro-crystals are perfectly uniform. Each structure is structurally identical at the sub-micron scale. No variance. No DEFECTS. It has completely altered the interior path of the corpse. In nature there are always defects.

P1: Doctor.

P3: In NATURE, agent, there are ALWAYS defects. This isn't a natural death.

P1: Stop the tape.

>> D. SESSION 3 – 23:55 PST

P1: Last session for tonight. Ranger, you mentioned a group of young people on the trail earlier this summer.

P2: Yeah. Teenagers. Maybe fifteen, sixteen. Showing up at the trailhead with backpacks and CD players.

P1: Did you speak with them?

P2: Once. In July. I asked them what they were doing out past the tree line. They told me they were "listening for the signal." That's a quote. "Listening for the signal."

P1: Did they appear intoxicated?

P2: No, sir. They appeared HAPPY. Unnaturally so. The older kid told me they found something left deep in the woods. A disk. Said they booted it up on a portable rig and... someone talked back to them through it.

P1: Someone talked back? Identify the speaker.

P2: They called him —--. The way they spoke about him... it was like he was a messenger from god himself. They've formed some kind of cult around it. They told me they set up a "page" on the internet to expand the group worldwide. They said they made seven copies of the disk to spread the word, but they hid the original back out in the brush where they first found it. They tried to hand me one of the copies. I threw it out.

P1: You threw a copy out. Where?

P2: In the ranger station dumpster. Last July.

P1: We will need to recover that dumpster immediately.

P2: Agent, it's been emptied a hundred times. Besides, it's not the copy you need to worry about. Hikers and wanderers keep coming down the east fork. They're finding the original disk out there in the woods and passing it around to other people. It's moving.

P1: [Static] Sector-wide quarantine protocols for —------  are now compromised.

P2: ...what are you going to do to those kids?

P1: End of session. Tape off.
